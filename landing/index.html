The game

Build your way, city by city

Paper Voyager is a lane runner: dodge obstacles, collect coins and reach the airport to fly to your next destination. Everything is crafted like a cut-paper storybook.

72 real destinations

From Osaka to Angel Falls, across 46 countries. Every city has its landmarks, weather and obstacles.

8 travelers, 8 stories

Each one travels for a reason. Follow their postcards and unlock their secret outfit.

Your travel backpack

Selfies in every city, souvenirs, passports for each region and an album to decorate.

Quick runs

A trip takes a couple of minutes, perfect for the bus. In 8 languages.
